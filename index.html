DecorScope Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026

DecorScope is a room decor preview app that lets users apply wallpaper, floor, and surface visuals inside a 3D room scene. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes and how that information is handled.

1. Information DecorScope Processes

DecorScope is designed to work primarily on the user’s device. Depending on how the app is used, the app may process:

  • Photos the user captures with the camera for decor sampling.
  • Photos the user selects from the device gallery.
  • Decor images created from those photos and saved by the user locally on the device.
  • Room preview settings, including selected decor assignments for walls, floor, and ceiling.
  • Exported preview images when the user chooses to save or share a result.

2. How DecorScope Uses This Information

DecorScope uses this information only to provide its core functionality:

  • Capture or import a decor reference image.
  • Crop and align decor samples.
  • Store user-created decor samples on the device.
  • Apply selected decor samples to room surfaces in the preview scene.
  • Export or share a final room preview image at the user’s request.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

DecorScope does not upload user decor photos, preview images, or room configurations to the developer’s servers.

DecorScope does not sell user data and does not share user data with advertisers or analytics providers.

When the user chooses to export or share a room preview, the image is shared only through the device’s operating system share flow and only to the destination selected by the user.

4. Local Storage

User-created decor samples and room state are stored locally on the device so the user can continue using them later. The app does not require a cloud account to use these features.

5. Permissions

DecorScope may request access to:

  • Camera to capture decor reference images.
  • Photos / media library to import decor images from the device.
  • Temporary file access / cache to export and share preview images.

These permissions are used only to support the features described in this policy.

6. Third-Party Services

DecorScope relies on operating-system level Android features and the Capacitor runtime to access camera, file, and share functionality. DecorScope does not include advertising SDKs and is not designed to transmit user decor content to external analytics services.

7. Children’s Privacy

DecorScope is not specifically directed to children under 13. The app is intended for general home-decor visualization by teen and adult users.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because data is stored locally on the device, users can delete their saved decor samples inside the app and can remove remaining local app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If the policy changes, the updated version should be published at the same public URL with a revised effective date.
